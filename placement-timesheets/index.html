Placement & Timesheet Management

Opportunity

Connect recruitment placements with contractor time entry, client approval and operational oversight, replacing disconnected exchanges with a shared workflow.

My ownership

I worked on the system and evolved its functionality over several years. The placement connected the candidate and job with dates, contacts, time periods and approval requirements.

Original workflow

Contractors entered time through a portal. Submission triggered notifications and approval by the configured contact or manager. Disputes required reasons, agreement and final sign-off. Activity and changes were recorded against the process.

Modernised presentation

This fictional reconstruction demonstrates an administration workspace and a mobile-style contractor portal. Commercial rates are stored as placement settings. Payroll, tax, invoice and payment calculations are outside the sample.

Proposed evolution

The purple controls demonstrate a proposed approach to corrections: retain the submitted version, create a revision, show the changes and obtain fresh agreement before final manager approval. Old approvals do not carry forward to a revised sheet.

A shared in-app inbox and email delivery visibility demonstrate how users could follow progress without relying on email alone. All notifications and login activity here are simulated.

How to explore

Open the mobile contractor preview, enter time and submit. Switch to the client contact to approve or contest it. For a correction, create a revision as the contractor, save and resubmit, then obtain approver agreement and final manager sign-off. Inspect the version history and notification log.

Availability

Contractors can record regular working times and date exceptions. An illustrative placement search uses those entries to identify availability for a requested date and time.
