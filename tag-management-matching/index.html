Tag Management & Two-Way Matching

Opportunity

Create a consistent way to organise, search and compare jobs and candidate records across the recruitment platform.

My ownership

I defined the tag structure, management functions and user workflows, worked with engineering on implementation and reviewed delivery through detailed testing and feedback.

The delivered foundation

The system combined a shared tag library with customer-specific tags, organised through parent–child relationships. Categories could be activated for relevant sectors. Tags with identical names retained separate identities and category context.

Location, travel radius, sector and other preferences established the search. Tags supported matching between jobs and candidate records in both directions, with users able to review and adjust their selections.

Evolution of the approach

This reconstruction also explores how I would develop that foundation using ideas from my AI-assisted job-matching project. Job requirements and candidate information would be structured separately, then compared using both explicit criteria and supporting evidence.

A central function would manage the core vocabulary and assessment criteria. Individual jobs would retain their own requirements and priorities, while candidates would retain their evidence and preferences. Tags would connect the information and support suggestions as new records are added.

Explaining a match

The proposed assessment would show which requirement was considered, the evidence supporting the match and anything that remained uncertain. The screening recommendation and ranking would be the outcome of that assessment. A reviewer could inspect the reasoning from either the job or the candidate view.

Equivalent wording could map to the same tag. Contextual interpretation could suggest a related capability, with the source passage available for review and inferred information clearly distinguished from confirmed evidence.

What this sample shows

The interface modernises the management and matching workflows using fictional records. Search preferences, editable tags and priorities, category activation and tag-based rankings work within this browser demonstration.

CV-to-tag extraction and evidence-based interpretation are presented as proposed extensions. CV passages and suggestions are authored examples; the wording tester demonstrates local phrase rules. No live parser or AI service is connected. Detailed assessment rules and scoring mechanics are outside this sample.

How to explore

Start with Jobs or Candidates, open a record and inspect its matches. The purple Proposed evolution tab presents an illustrative requirement-by-requirement evidence assessment. Adjust preferences or tags to see the results change. Review a candidate’s supporting passage, then explore the three-tier library and related wording in Tag management.

Scope

Talent pool management, invitations and application monitoring are separate projects. This sample focuses on the tagging foundation and its potential evolution into evidence-based matching.
