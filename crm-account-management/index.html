Customer account visibility and renewals

I developed a CRM and intranet to manage jobs, tickets, renewals, account management, scheduling and timesheets. The later CRM was an architectural upgrade to that platform. This sample focuses on the separate account area, customer dashboard and data management.

My approach
I specified changes in phases and milestones, using manageable increments that could fit around available development capacity and urgent work. The wider specification went beyond what was delivered.
The requirement
Record sales and renewals separately, link each to a customer and job, expose customer value and renewal information, and allow staff to find and export the records.
One shared workspace
The aim was to centralise customer records, call notes and follow-up so staff worked from shared information rather than separate Outlook accounts and spreadsheets. Email parsing was a development priority, not a delivered capability.
What I learned
The usefulness of a management system depends on the quality of its data. Reduced Customer Services capacity and limited engagement with maintaining records constrained the value of the reporting.

Reconstructed interface based on surviving briefs and my account. Layout, calculations, controls and fictional examples are prepared for this portfolio, not original screenshots. Values are record totals, not an accounting ledger or a revenue recognition model. Changes save only in this browser. No original client data or branding is used.

Brian Turner · Project information

Intranet / CRM Business Management Platform

Initial platform: ZODNET

ZODNET, our internally developed CRM and intranet, was designed and delivered over several years to meet the changing requirements of a growing company.

My ownership

I led the development of ZODNET, defining its scope, functionality and workflows as the business grew, and working with our engineering team to deliver and refine the platform. It brought together customer management, quotations, jobs, support tickets, scheduling and timesheets, alongside invoicing, recurring services, renewals and operational reporting.

Outcome

Created a shared operational platform that reduced administration, improved visibility across the business and supported company growth.

CRM upgrade

The later upgrade modernised the platform’s architecture and enhanced the wider system, improving how teams managed customer relationships, commercial information and day-to-day work.

I shaped and specified the upgrade, designed workflows and interfaces, and oversaw development and testing. Enhancements included expanded job management, a separate account area, customer dashboards, commercial reporting and export, and integration with an independent ticket system. These changes built on ZODNET’s existing capabilities and were delivered in phases around business priorities and development capacity.

Results and learning

The upgrade improved access to customer, operational and commercial information, bringing account values, renewals and support activity into a clearer shared view. The delivered upgrade formed part of a broader development plan, with consistent data entry and staff adoption remaining essential to its value.

About this demonstration

This AI-assisted reconstruction focuses on the account area, customer dashboard and data management. The interface and example data are illustrative; it is not the original application or source code.
