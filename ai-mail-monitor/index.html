AI Mail Monitor & Application Engine

Opportunity

Job searching involves repeated alerts, duplicate vacancies, incomplete information and considerable time assessing roles before an application can begin.

My approach

I developed an application-engine approach to assess roles against career evidence, work preferences and screening requirements. The aim is to explain decisions, preserve a person's voice and avoid unsupported claims in application documents.

The proposed product

A mail monitor would identify vacancies within alerts, check the source, remove duplicates and create a structured list. The engine would ask focused questions, assess the roles and present a shortlist before preparing documents for review.

What already exists

The assessment approach, evidence controls and writing rules are used in my own application process. This does not mean the mailbox-to-application workflow is already automated.

What this sample demonstrates

This click-through storyboard illustrates the intended experience. Email layout examples are informed by anonymised structure studies of supplied alerts. Source checks, extraction outputs, scores and generated wording remain scripted fictional examples. No inbox access, real source verification or AI inference takes place.

Design decisions

Keep fit, personal preference and screening strength separate. Ask when evidence is missing. Explain both positive and negative decisions. Let the person challenge the assessment and approve the application draft.

Continuing development

The walkthrough is a starting point for defining the automation, integrations and exception handling needed to finish the wider product.
